About Me
Amanuel is a seasoned Data Management Professional with over seven years of experience. Currently serving as a Data Manager at FDRE Policy Studies Institute (PSI) and Addis Economic Research PLC (AER), he has a proven track record in data collection, field data quality supervision, data collection tool programming, and data management. Amanuel holds a Bachelor of Arts in Geography and Environmental Studies from Addis Ababa University.

His expertise has been developed through diverse research projects with renowned organizations such as PSI (Policy Studies Institute), CSA (Central Statistics Agency, Formerly), Oxford Martin Programme on the Future of Development at the University of Oxford, New York University Abu Dhabi, WB, IFPRI, ILRI, CIMMYT, IFAD, JSI-L10K, New York University, Arba Minch University, and KU Leuven. Amanuel is an expert at adopting cutting-edge technologies and methodologies and is unwavering committed to ensuring data accuracy, efficiency, and reliability. His extensive experience has given him a comprehensive understanding of data management principles and best practices.

In his previous roles, he demonstrated his ability to manage large datasets, ensuring its integrity and accuracy. He has a proven track record of designing and programming effective data collection tools for Computer-Assisted Personal Interviewing (CAPI) and Computer-Assisted Telephone Interviewing (CATI), which have significantly improved the efficiency of data collection processes.
